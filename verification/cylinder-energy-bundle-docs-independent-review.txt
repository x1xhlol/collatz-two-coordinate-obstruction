Reviewed README.md and SOURCE-LICENSES.txt against the exact source definitions,
main endpoint proofs, bundle inventory, retained provenance records, and the
prerequisite license descriptions. This is a documentation/semantic-scope
review, not a replacement for the separate portable verifier audit or replay.

The strict head 0<N<3^K, odd-target condition, and inclusive return exclusion
1<=j<=K are accurate. The finite mask can include periodic targets with period
larger than K. The first-hit coefficient theorem covers positive exact depths
and periodic targets; transfer to full cylinder density requires this mask.
The older nonperiodic energy has the inclusive endpoint 3^K. The sufficiency
proof removes that endpoint using zero actual cylinder mass for K>0.

The energy is a finite sum of actual (canonicalRho K N/N)^2. The stated mass
budget 2+3K+(64/81)^K(1+2K) is the actual first-power mass estimate for positive
K, and the README correctly distinguishes it from limiting basin densities.
The two equivalences assert universal eventual periodicity of positive
shortcut orbits, not convergence to the known cycle or uniqueness of cycles.
The barrier function is any real-valued M(K) tending to +infinity. Only the
normalized no-small-ancestor portion vanishes unconditionally; decay of the
remaining small-ancestor portion is the equivalent open criterion.

The manifest contains exactly 17 application modules (five coefficient,
four finite transport/energy, six older implication, two equivalence), 40
reused periodic-census modules, and 1,566 reused native modules. All 17 supplied
source hashes match both the research sources and their provenance entries;
the four historical replay-record digests match. The manifest's file hashes
all match at review time. The README accurately describes additive replay and
reuse of Lean/Mathlib/native caches, without claiming fresh checking of the
entire imported closure. Detailed verifier behavior is separately audited.

MIT attribution to Lucas Valbuena is retained for original local material.
External Gao and predecessor-derived material retains its upstream Apache-2.0
attribution and notices in the prerequisites. No external source is relicensed
