orbit. Its extra coefficient is D_u/(P_infinity(u)*log(3/2)); positivity
additionally uses a unit root. The residual is nonnegative on every
source and its full source mean is retained, while pre-barrier and
forward-prefix contributions are disjoint. The written order of limits
and logarithmic prefix bound agree with the formal endpoint. No
injective orbit is asserted to exist.

2. The inverse-clock theorem concerns the actual continuing law at a
fixed positive odd nonperiodic unit root. Nonperiodicity means no

infer convergence of expected occupation from almost-sure growth.
