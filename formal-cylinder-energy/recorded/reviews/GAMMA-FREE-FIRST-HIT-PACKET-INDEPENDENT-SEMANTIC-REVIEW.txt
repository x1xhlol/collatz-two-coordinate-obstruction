1. Exact proved conclusion and numerical constant

The packet defines a_k(N) as the sum over positive odd sources whose
ACTUAL FIRST shortcut hit of N has exactly k odd steps. Its nonzero
summands equal N*firstHitWeight N q/q, or equivalently 3^k/2^A for the
actual first-hit shortcut time A.

For one native global b>0 with D_q>=b/q at all positive unit q, it proves

  a_k(N) <= (64/b)*N*D_N

for every positive odd N and k>=1, including periodic targets. The
exported existential theorem chooses the same constant C=64/b before
quantifying over all targets and depths. Oddness already gives N>0.

The actual cylinder bound rho_k(N)<=C*N*D_N additionally assumes

  Syracuse^j(N)!=N for every 1<=j<=k.

This includes all odd nonperiodic targets and periodic targets whose
least odd period exceeds k. A return exactly at k is excluded by the
mask. The packet does not bound unrestricted all-hit coefficients on
short-cycle targets by D_N.

The formal constant is 64/b. The analytic report's sharper 64/(3b)
is NOT the constant proved by this implementation. The factor-three
loss is explained by the valid larger exponent sum in section 2 below.

2. Finite fan coefficient estimate

finite_syracuse_fan_geometric_bound takes a finite collection of odd
sources with the same Syracuse successor. Their exponents are positive
and are distinct by syracuse_predecessor_exponent_injective. It compares
their sum to the full geometric series over ALL positive exponents:

  sum_Q 2^(-a(q)) <= sum_(a>=1)2^(-a)=1.

The code inserts exponent zero into the finite exponent set, compares
to the full nonnegative geometric tsum (which equals 2), and subtracts
the zero-exponent term. Positivity of odd-source exponents justifies
that insertion as a disjoint extra term.

finite_firstHit_fan_bound uses the exact first-hit successor coefficient
identity lambda(q)=3*2^(-a(q))*lambda(y). The parent coefficient is
nonnegative, so the finite fan mass is at most 3*lambda(y).

short_predecessor_coefficient_bound uses a(q*)<=6, hence
2^(-a(q*))>=1/64, and the same exact coefficient identity. It proves

  3*lambda(y)<=64*lambda(q*).

Combining these two inequalities gives the factor 64 from each finite
fan to its selected representative. No parity-residue improvement of
the geometric series is asserted in this formal bound.

3. Selected representatives are a genuine finite cut

For an arbitrary finite collection Q of exact depth-(k+1) first-hit
sources, the proof forms the FINITE parent image Y=Syracuse(Q).
Every parent in Y is positive odd and not divisible by 3. The selected
leaf theorem therefore supplies a unit nonperiodic odd predecessor
q* of that parent with exponent at most 6.

The original source's first-hit predicate gives the parent's exact
first-hit depth k. syrFirstHit_prepend_nonperiodic gives a genuine
depth-(k+1) first hit from q*. In particular q*=N is ruled out by a
positive return to q*, rather than being silently included as a
positive-depth first hit at a periodic target.

The selected leaf need not belong to Q. Distinct parents nevertheless
give distinct selected leaves because a selected leaf has exactly its
specified Syracuse successor. The proof establishes this injection
before replacing a sum over parents by a sum over the selected image P.

Each member of P is converted to an actual shortcut FirstHit at its
native clock, with oddCount=k+1. The finite cut-capacity theorem then
applies to P. Its nonperiodic hypothesis means no positive return at
the leaf; it does NOT mean that the leaf never eventually enters a
cycle. Thus its use is valid for periodic endpoints as well.

Fiberwise summation of Q by its first successor, the factor-64 fan
comparison, and cut capacity give

  sum_Q lambda(q)<=64*N*D_N/b.

Only the selected LEAVES need be units. No unit-modulo-three assumption
on N appears in the final theorem. For odd N divisible by 3, a positive
depth leaf cannot exist; the same finite proof remains valid.

4. Infinite coefficient and density normalizations

exactFirstHitCoefficient_le_density begins with native-backed
summability of the exact term. To bound an arbitrary finite partial
sum it filters that finite set to its nonzero exact-depth odd sources,
uses the exact shortcut/Syracuse first-hit equivalence, and applies
the uniform finite theorem just proved. The summable tsum is then
bounded by the same number.

This is a finite-partial-sum argument on nonnegative coefficients.
It makes no countable-additivity claim about natural density and does
not take a source-mean limit uniformly in a moving target or depth.

The capacity theorem uses full-source logarithmic means tending to
actualFirstHitDensity, and the native reciprocal floor is stated for
that same density. Oddness restricts the LEAF coefficient sum, not
the source mean used to measure a selected leaf's full basin. There
is no omitted odd-source factor of two in the conversion.

5. Final exported theorems discharge the floor

exists_uniform_odd_exact_firstHit_coefficient_bound obtains the native
exists_uniform_firstHit_reciprocal_floor once and sets C=64/b>0.
Any positive k is rewritten as j+1 and the exact coefficient estimate
is applied. The bound has no residual unproved floor assumption.

exists_uniform_odd_masked_cylinder_bound makes the same native choice
and uses the independently reviewed finite-no-return reconstruction
to replace rho_k by a_k. It does not assert that all targets satisfy
that mask. No approximation of a cycle factor is involved.

6. Assumption and axiom audit scope

The source contains no bounded harmonic-target assumption, no uniform
rho/g majorant, no Green cycle-factor bound, no eventual-periodicity
assumption, and no inference from cumulative Cesaro convergence to a
pointwise coefficient estimate. The only infinite coefficient passage
uses already proved summability plus finite bounds.
