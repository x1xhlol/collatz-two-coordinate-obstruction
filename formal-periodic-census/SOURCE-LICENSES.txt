provenance/gao/. The archive contains no separate NOTICE file.

The only compatibility changes affecting Lean imports are the replacement
of Mathlib.Analysis.Real.Sqrt by Mathlib.Analysis.SpecialFunctions.Sqrt
in Momentos.lean and Hienas.lean. Each supplied modified file carries a
prominent comment describing that change and the packaging notice.

patch, original hashes, previous compatible hashes and supplied hashes are
retained. The other 24 Gao source files are byte-identical to the archive.
The archive also contains four Garner/Wu modules outside the Gao closure;
they are preserved in the original ZIP but are not application sources
compiled by this bundle.

Predecessor-derived work

BoundedFrozenSeed.lean adapts Lech Mazur's GeneralTargetFrozenSeed proof.
GenericSeedPredecessorCount.lean and GenericPowerPredecessorCount.lean
adapt his GeneralTargetPositiveDensity count assembly. Their existing
source notices identify the retained arguments and changed seed/terminal
parameters; those source bytes and notices are preserved.

FrozenSeedPeriodicPrefix.lean reuses the bounded seed/generation assembly,
and TerminalFanCensus.lean follows the predecessor terminal census
interfaces and argument. Their derivation is acknowledged here along
with the new fixed periodic-envelope and basin-limit construction. These
adaptations retain all rights in Mazur's predecessor-derived material;
they are not described as wholly unrelated MIT code. Original local
additions retain the Lucas Valbuena MIT attribution.

Lech Mazur's predecessor release is licensed under Apache License 2.0.
The exact license and notice supplied with the bounded-seed extension are
retained as licenses/LICENSE-PREDECESSOR-APACHE-2.0 and
licenses/NOTICE-PREDECESSOR. The retained native baseline license, notice,
rights explanation and grant metadata are in provenance/bounded-seed/.
Existing copyright and license headers have not been removed.
