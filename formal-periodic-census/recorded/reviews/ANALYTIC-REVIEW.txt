2. Frozen seed and terminal scale

exists_bounded_generalTarget_uniform_twoThirds_seed places its existential root
r before the universal quantifier over all sufficiently large later generations
and their admissible physical scales. Its bound is r <= K_seed*a, with K_seed
fixed by the initial base, initial generation and initial mixing constant.
Setting ordinary target a=2N gives r <= 2 K_seed N. Terminal conductor m does
not occur in this seed selection. Consequently the same r can be held fixed
while m tends to infinity after the source-size limit.

The natural-number seed bound and the nonreturning property are both carried by
the bounded theorem. The older unbounded residue-coverage proof alone would
not supply r <= K_seed*a, so the bounded replacement is a material input.

For each fixed m the interval-cover argument used in
generalTarget_publicCount_from_mass allows increasing the starting generation
before selecting a generation for each sufficiently large X. This is enough
to ensure k>=m; no simultaneous rate in m and X is needed. Positive marked mass
supplies 3^k<=X through the existing conductor-room theorem.

4. Coarse fan and error

At fixed m, h_m is a bounded function on the finite residue space. The uniformly
convergent series P_m(q)=sum_j 4^(-j) h_m(F_j(q)) has period 3^m and mean 8/9,
because every residue fan F_j is a permutation and mean(h_m)=2/3.
The finite J-term fan is bounded above by this same P_m, independently of J.

Replacing h_k by h_m leaves an absolute error with uniform ternary mean at most
epsilon_m=C_mix/m^2. For each j, the existing weighted residue census bounds
the error by 33 r epsilon_m. Multiplication by the geometric coefficients and
their sum at most 4/3 gives 44 r epsilon_m. Replacing the good subweight v by
the full weight W is valid because 0<=v<=W and all coarse/error terms are
nonnegative. This reproduces the source inequalities in
GeneralTargetTerminalCensus without its pointwise-height loss 3^m.

5. Natural periodic census and constants

For a fixed positive target N, common-clock adjacent coalescence implies
sublinear adjacent variation of 1_(B_N): outside the periodic bad edges and
the finite set of starts hitting N by the common clock, the two membership
values agree. The early-hit exception has target-uniform finite cardinality
at each fixed clock. Periodic block cancellation then gives decorrelation
from every fixed centered periodic test. Combining this with the established
ordinary natural density d_N yields the required natural periodic source mean.
The weighted note's logarithmic endpoint is not being used as a substitute for
this natural mean; the unweighted variation proof supplies it directly.

Because 3^m is odd, odd(q)*P_m(q) has period 2*3^m and mean (1/2)(8/9)=4/9.
Therefore, for fixed m,

  (r/X) sum_(q<32X) 1_(B_N)(q) odd(q) P_m(q)
      -> (128r/9) d_N.

The marked lower bound consequently gives

  2/3 <= (128r/9) d_N + 44 r epsilon_m.

Only after this source limit do we send m to infinity, keeping r fixed. The
error vanishes and the arithmetic is

  d_N >= (2/3)(9/(128r)) = 3/(64r)
      >= 3/(128 K_seed N).

Allowing the norm of P_m and the convergence threshold to grow with m is
harmless in this ordered argument. There is no exchange of the two limits.

6. Boundaries

This argument gives an absolute density floor if formally assembled. It does
not capture a prescribed fraction of the whole basin with the frozen packet.
Two one-sided packet-to-basin inequalities cannot be divided to obtain the
required relative sibling bound. No marked occupation estimate, cross-root
absolute continuity, exclusion of divergence or cycle exclusion follows from
this audit. It also makes no claim that the new analytic assembly was already
Lean-checked when the candidate was written.
