Semantic review of the periodic-census polynomial basin-density floor
3 October 2026

Result and scope

I found no mathematical gap in the new candidate's polynomial-floor argument
or in its implementation in the seven modules listed below. The final theorem
proves, for the existing actual shortcut basin density d_N,

  3 / (128 * predecessorSeedMultiplier * N) <= d_N

for every natural N with 0 < N and N % 3 != 0. The multiplier is the fixed,
positive, target-independent integer defined in BoundedPredecessorDensity.
This is an absolute density floor. It is not a proof of Collatz, a comparison
between two different basin densities, or the requested inverse-logarithmic
relative sibling estimate.

1. Freeze one seed before choosing the terminal conductor

The quantifier order in exists_bounded_generalTarget_uniform_twoThirds_seed
is sufficient. At ordinary target 2*N, with predecessorSeedBase,
predecessorSeedGeneration and explicitSyracuseMixingCoefficient fixed once,
it gives one positive odd seed r satisfying r <= K_seed*(2*N), reaching
ordinary target 2*N and having no positive Syracuse return. The same seed
has good marked mass at least 2/3 at every sufficiently large generation
and every admissible physical scale. The existential seed is outside the
universal later-generation statement and outside the later choice of m.

FrozenSeedPeriodicPrefix preserves this order exactly. It chooses r first,
then takes an arbitrary m >= 1. Its generation threshold
Gmark + 2*m + 20*10^9 provides the quarter-conductor room. For every integer
X above a finite m-dependent height, interval coverage gives an admissible
generation n, which is allowed to depend on X. The positive marked mass
gives 3^k <= X at the actual terminal conductor k. Thus the census hypotheses
are met for every sufficiently large X, not just for a subsequence. No
threshold depending on m is retained in the eventual density coefficient.

The fixed seed multiplier may be enormous. Its numerical size is irrelevant
to this quantifier issue: it is independent of target N and terminal m.

2. Keep a fixed bounded periodic fan

For fixed m, terminalFanEnvelope sums the actual residue fan h_m(F_j(y))
against 4^(-j), where h_m is the full ternary reference-density function.
Nonnegativity and the finite height bound (2/3)*3^m prove summability,
finite-fan domination, and the bound (8/9)*3^m. The fan residue map is a
permutation for every j, so it preserves the normalized full ternary mean.
The imported mean of h_m is 2/3. Exchanging the finite residue sum and the
summable series therefore gives the envelope mean (4/3)*(2/3) = 8/9.

This is the full ternary mean, not a mean conditioned on unit residues.
OddTerminalFanMean correctly pairs q and q+3^m in one period 2*3^m.
Because 3^m is odd, exactly one member of each pair is odd, and both have
the same residue modulo 3^m. The odd-masked envelope consequently has
ordinary mean 4/9. Its period, mean and nonnegativity are all implemented
explicitly. No fan depth growing with X enters the periodic-mean theorem.

3. High-conductor error and multiplicity

TerminalFanCensus applies the existing weighted residue census to the
absolute high/low-conductor difference after each fan permutation. The
uniform L1 error is epsilon = C_mix/m^2 for every k >= m. Each fan term
costs at most 33*r*epsilon for the singleton seed state. The geometric
coefficient sum is at most 4/3, giving precisely 44*r*epsilon.

The code first bounds the good-core subweight by the original incidence
weight; the fan values are nonnegative, which is the needed direction.
It then replaces the finite fixed-m fan by the nonnegative envelope.
It does not equate these incidence weights with unrelated first-hit or
canonical correction weights.

The imported fullTerminal_weighted_source_charge_of_nonreturningSeed
allows an arbitrary nonnegative source test. Its proof uses the seed's
nonreturn condition to eliminate repeated incidence multiplicity before
passing to the finite set of physical sources. The new code supplies the
envelope as that test and obtains r/X times its source sum. I checked the
source-charge proof's nonreturn hypothesis, equal hitting-time argument,
and word/source injectivity; this is not a hidden bounded-multiplicity
assumption in the new derivation.

4. Extend sources to the actual shortcut basin

Every charged source is positive and odd, lies below 32*X, and reaches r
under the ordinary predecessor map. The seed reaches ordinary 2*N.
NativePredecessorMapBridge converts this double-target reachability into
membership in the actual shortcut basin of N. This target conversion is
explicit in terminalEnvelope_source_sum_le_basin_prefix.

The finite source set is extended to the entire natural prefix q < 32*X,
weighted by basinIndicator N q and the odd mask. Every added summand is
nonnegative. The direction is an upper bound on the constructed packet;
it asserts no lower capture of the whole basin by that packet. In
particular, this step cannot be reversed or divided between two targets.
Including q=0 in the formal prefix makes no difference to the intended
positive-start sum because its odd mask is zero.

5. Periodic source averaging

PeriodicSourceMean proves the needed generic fact rather than assuming
independence from congruence classes. A centered periodic test has uniformly
bounded partial sums. Summation by parts bounds its weighted prefix sum by
C + C*adjacentVariation(w,X) when |w| <= 1. Dividing by X, sublinear
adjacent variation makes the centered contribution tend to zero. The
uncentered part uses the ordinary mean of w. The bound C may depend on the
fixed periodic test, its period, and its height; no uniformity in m is used.

ActualBasinPeriodicMean supplies the actual basin indicator's 0/1 bound,
its pre-existing natural density, and the actual-basin adjacent-variation
theorem from the disclosed bridge. It also proves the harmless shift from
the existing mean over q+1 to the mean over q in Finset.range X. Therefore
the generic conditional theorem becomes an unconditional theorem for the
existing actualBasinDensity, with no remaining decorrelation hypothesis.

For fixed m and fixed r, OddTerminalFanMean composes the mean limit with
X |-> 32*X and checks the scale conversion. The resulting limit is

  (r/X) * sum_(q<32X) basinIndicator(N,q)*oddTerminalFan(m,q)
    -> (128*r/9)*d_N.

The factor 128/9 is 32 times the odd-masked mean 4/9. There is no missing
parity factor or denominator normalization.

6. Take the two limits in the correct order

ActualBasinPolynomialFloor first fixes m >= 1 and takes X to infinity in
the eventual prefix inequality. Its use of ge_of_tendsto preserves

  2/3 <= (128*r/9)*d_N + 44*r*C_mix/m^2.

It then lets m tend to infinity with the same r, C_mix and N still fixed.
The remaining error tends to zero. This proves d_N >= 3/(64*r), and
r <= 2*K_seed*N gives the stated 3/(128*K_seed*N) floor. The positivity
conditions needed for division follow from the seed's strict positivity.
The final target assumptions are exactly 0 < N and N % 3 != 0.

The source and conductor limits are not interchanged. Neither uniform
source convergence in m nor any effective convergence rate is needed.

Verification and remaining boundary

At the source hashes in FULL-SEMANTIC-REVIEW.json, the available compile
logs for all nine new modules report only propext, Classical.choice and
Quot.sound for the printed declarations; in particular the final
actual_basin_polynomial_lower_density closure contains no sorryAx or
added analytic axiom. The final endpoint's successful local compilation
was observed directly. I ran the separate kernel checker on my two bridge
modules; those runs exited successfully and are recorded in
gao-bridge-replay.json. I did not run a fresh full closure replay for all
seven independently reviewed modules as part of this semantic review.
Any root-run complete replay should be reported separately and bound to
its own manifest.

The candidate text retains its original scratch-status sentences saying
that formalization is not yet complete. Those sentences describe the state
when the candidate was written, not the later compiled endpoint reviewed
here. The text itself has been left unchanged. Optional packing-weight
and canonical-scalar corollaries mentioned there are not conclusions of
ActualBasinPolynomialFloor and are outside this review's formal endpoint.

No claim is made that the absolute floor supplies the missing whole-basin
relative comparison, removes nontrivial cycles, proves every orbit reaches
one, or solves Collatz.
