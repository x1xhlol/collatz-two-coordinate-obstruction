Source licenses and attribution

This ledger covers the 362 Lean source modules in source-manifest.json.
The source origins and hashes are fixed by provenance/source-origins.json.

87 original local modules use the MIT license in licenses/LICENSE-MIT:
43 retained native modules, 26 retained private modules, and 18 new fair-Haar
modules. Copyright (c) 2026 Lucas Valbuena. The exact module classification is
recorded in provenance/origin-license-review.json; upstream sources are excluded
from this MIT scope.

273 source/Erdos1135/ modules derive from Lech Mazur's pinned Tao/natural-density
formalization, package ca3dd0d63920411213403092aecc6946619eb082. Of these, 241
remain byte-identical to the upstream source-manifest hashes and the retained
native bundle at Git revision 7ef1208600107e62561ad6090c06a8cd4e0a1143. The other
32 are reviewed local compatibility adaptations: their original bytes match
those same upstream and retained-source pins, and their current bytes and exact
patches are recorded in provenance/native-linter-patches.json. They update proof
tactics and spellings, retain named arguments through local references, and in
three files alpha-rename five unused bound-variable occurrences inside result
types. Each adapted file carries a modification notice; definitions and logical
statements are preserved. Their Apache-2.0 license and Advameg notice are retained
in licenses/LICENSE-APACHE-2.0 and licenses/NOTICE-ADVAMEG. No local MIT grant is
applied to these upstream files. See provenance/upstream-selected-source-pins.json
for separate unchanged and adapted source lists with original, current and patch
hashes.

The full corrected inventory is 18 new local modules, 285 unchanged retained
native modules, 32 adapted retained native modules, 26 retained private modules,
and one reviewed source shard. The declared license totals remain 87 MIT and
275 Apache-2.0. Records under provenance/original-attempt/ describe the preserved
original attempt and are not current-byte validation for this corrected bundle.

Two source/FormalConjectures/ modules retain their full copyright and Apache
headers. See licenses/NOTICE-FORMAL-CONJECTURES for the compatibility shim and
the exact modified Wikipedia shard. The latter's deletion patch and original
source retrieval information are frozen in provenance/wikipedia-shard.*.

The predecessor and second-scale namespaces and M. Sharpe's adapted parity
module from the larger native bundle are not present in this selected closure.
Their licenses are not repurposed as grants for this selection.

Mathlib and the eight pinned Lake packages remain external dependencies. Their
source and compiled caches are not distributed in this bundle. Exact upstream
license copies are supplied under licenses/packages/, with repository revisions
in provenance/external-license-origins.json. Lean itself is an external official
toolchain pinned in dependency-pins.json; no toolchain archive or runtime is
part of this source bundle. The license inventory here covers this bundle's
source and the named dependency license files, not every transitive asset of
an external toolchain or package.

Mathematical papers are cited as mathematical sources and are not reproduced
under these software grants. No external manuscript or image is included in
this source-license scope.
