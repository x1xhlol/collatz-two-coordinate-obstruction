Source licenses and attribution: canonical integer-envelope bundle

This ledger covers the 1620 Lean source modules selected by the frozen
execution manifest c3a623f8f06ec66824099ca3b4e40e5448533c0dfb0ce389cee70e52879dc28c.
The per-module hashes, source origins, declared license scopes and supporting
records are in provenance/envelope-license-review.json.

The selected sources comprise 276 MIT-scoped modules,
1340 Apache-2.0-scoped modules, and four predecessor-derived
modules whose upstream Apache-2.0 rights and MIT original local additions
are recorded separately. These are source-scope classifications; the local
MIT grant is not applied to imported or adapted third-party material.

Original local proof modules, scripts and documentation use
licenses/LICENSE-MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Lucas Valbuena. FiniteBinomialTail
and OrbitPackingParityCount retain M. Sharpe's separate MIT copyright and
full permission notice in their source headers, at the recorded upstream
commit ec8174b567d5cab4960024782210b5f5db02bd3a.

The Erdos1135, Erdos1135SecondScale, Erdos1135Predecessor,
FormalConjectures and FormalConjecturesPredecessor namespaces, together
with CollatzPredecessorDensity, retain their upstream rights and notices.
Lech Mazur's first-passage/natural-density package and predecessor release
are distinct retained sources. Their licenses, notices, namespace changes
and predecessor rights records are preserved in licenses/ and
provenance/retained-native/. The selected second-scale and predecessor
copies remain Apache-2.0 material. Existing headers are unchanged.

There are 150 selected proof-compatibility adaptations: the 32 earlier
sharp-bundle patches in provenance/native-linter-patches.json and the
118 additional compatibility patches in
provenance/envelope-linter-patches.json. Their exact original/current
hashes, patches and modification notices are retained. The separate
FormalConjectures Wikipedia shard has its own exact deletion patch and
provenance; it retains the definition and omits an unrelated unproved
conjecture declaration. Compatibility edits retain theorem propositions, allowing bound-variable alpha-renaming;
local evaluation limits are raised where needed. No linter or proof-check exemption is used.

Twenty-three selected Gao modules come from Omar Javier Said Duran's
formal artifact, version 1.0.0, 27 September 2026,
doi:10.5281/zenodo.23003500, Copyright 2026 Omar Javier Said Duran.
They remain Apache-2.0; see licenses/LICENSE-GAO-APACHE-2.0. The complete
retained source archive, original README, original Gao sources and exact
compatibility records are under
provenance/retained-periodic-census/provenance/gao/. Momentos retains the
recorded import compatibility adaptation. Additional proof compatibility
edits in Tejonas are recorded in provenance/envelope-linter-patches.json.
The archive's other sources are historical evidence, not additional
compiled modules. The retained archive contains no paper or image files.

BoundedFrozenSeed, GenericSeedPredecessorCount,
FrozenSeedPeriodicPrefix and TerminalFanCensus reuse or adapt predecessor
proof assemblies. Their upstream rights are retained under
licenses/LICENSE-PREDECESSOR-APACHE-2.0 and licenses/NOTICE-PREDECESSOR,
with supporting grant records under
provenance/retained-periodic-census/provenance/bounded-seed/.
Original local additions retain their local MIT attribution. The
retained census SOURCE-LICENSES.txt explains these derivations.

The copied licenses/SOURCE-LICENSES.txt and
provenance/origin-license-review.json describe the earlier 362-module
sharp bundle only. They are preserved as historical source-specific
evidence. Their earlier whole-bundle counts and statement excluding
second-scale/predecessor namespaces do not apply to this expanded bundle.
Use this ledger and provenance/envelope-license-review.json for the
current selection.

Lean, Mathlib and the eight pinned packages remain external dependencies;
their source trees and compiled caches are not distributed here. Exact

Papers and images cited by upstream packages are not reproduced or
relicensed by these software grants. The companion paper has its
