FiniteObservableEventLower correctly generalizes the earlier occupation
union estimate to an arbitrary observable nonnegative on odd sources.
Only odd sources enter its mean. A failed good-event conjunction supplies
at least one bad indicator; on the conjunction the stated lower bound
applies. The same finite cumulative calculation gives L*(1-2*sum d)<=A.
There is no independence assumption or assumption of individual event
means. A normalized mean is explicitly required for the observable and
is discharged by the next module for the actual residual.

ResidualOccupationSourceMean subtracts the entire root-basin future
observable firstHitWeight(u,q)*sum_F firstHitWeight(N,u) from the finite
occupation sum. Injectivity of the root's shortcut orbit and the stated
finite forward-target conditions make this residual nonnegative for all
sources q. For sources outside the root basin the subtracted term is
zero. Exact finite linearity and the native odd-source mean yield
sum_odd_N<=R D_N - D_u*sum_F firstHitWeight(N,u).
The target cutoff and finite F are fixed during this source limit.

AlignedResidualOccupationLower applies the previously checked disjoint
pre-barrier/post-root target inequality. Requiring odd u<=M puts the
root after the first successful M-passage. Shortcut injectivity prevents
its future from meeting the earlier pre-barrier target segment. The
same actual aligned clock events and finite confinement bound give the
baseline after subtraction. Clipping at zero uses global residual
nonnegativity; no subtraction of an uncontrolled bad-source contribution
is made.

UniformResidualDensityLower uses n+2 actual fixed ladder events and their
common-base failure envelope, exactly as the baseline packet does. The
union loss multiplies only the pre-barrier scalar lower value. Averaging
the nonnegative residual retains the WHOLE term D_u*sum_F weights when it
is added back. In particular no good-event mask or failure factor is
silently attached to that extra term. This is justified on every source,
including sources outside the good events. The native constants are
chosen before M, grid, cutoff, root and finite forward target set. Only
explicit finite grid budgets remain at the first endpoint.

ForwardOddPrefix uses strict increase of the shortcut clock to inherit
Syracuse injectivity. Its image of {0,...,J-1} therefore has J distinct
odd targets. The growth budget (3/2)^J*(u+1)<=R+1 places every such target
below R; use of J instead of J-1 is conservative and correct. Every target
is reached by the actual shortcut time. The native whole-orbit correction
factorization and lower bound 1 for each tail imply every forward
first-hit weight is at least 1/wholeOrbitCorrection(u). Thus the prefix
weight sum is at least J/wholeOrbitCorrection(u). The zero-length prefix
and depth-zero target are both handled correctly. The native correction
lemmas apply to positive roots with injective shortcut orbits; no periodic
orbit is accidentally included.

AsymptoticResidualWithPrefix accepts an explicit natural prefix length
J(R) with its two clearly stated elementary hypotheses: its normalized
length tends to a/log(3/2), and its forward-growth budget eventually holds.
With 0<a<1, the explicit power grid discharges the finite occupation grid
conditions. Choose n first; then choose ONE M above both the native cutoff
and u, making the fixed-M coefficient large enough; then let R grow.
The finite set F=oddForwardPrefix(u,J(R)) varies with R, but its source
mean was already taken separately for each finite R. There is no source
and target limit interchange. Nonnegative D_u permits replacement of the
future weight sum by J(R)/wholeOrbitCorrection(u). Division is performed
only for R>=2, where log R>0. The resulting eventual comparison holds for
every b below a/clockDrift + D_u*a/(log(3/2)*wholeOrbitCorrection(u)).
No finite-real liminf hypothesis appears.
