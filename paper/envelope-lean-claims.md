# Positive semicontinuous Syracuse density: Lean claim map

This map accompanies *A positive semicontinuous density for the Syracuse stationary law* by Lucas Valbuena. The envelope is constructed from the infima of that function over positive integers in each ternary residue class.

The envelope takes values in Lean's nonnegative extended reals, `ℝ≥0∞`. Its integer trace is `ENNReal.ofReal (actualDensityValue actualFirstHitDensity n)`; nonnegativity of the actual real trace is separately proved, so the conversion does not discard a negative part. Positive integer equality has only the hypothesis `0 < n`. It includes even integers, multiples of three, nonperiodic integers that later enter a cycle, and periodic integers. The measure is full additive Haar probability on `ℤ_[3]`; Haar probability is not renormalized on the units.

The minimum-mass definition is directly in terms of `T.syracPMF` and the finite set of residues not divisible by three. Its depth-zero value is defined to be one. The nontrivial uniform inequalities quantify over every depth `k ≥ 1`. The constant is existential, uniform in the depth and residue, and is not asserted to be optimal.

The generic layer theorem is written on `ℕ`, with superharmonicity required only at positive arguments. An arbitrary nonnegative function on positive integers extends at zero by zero; the first-hit sum into a positive target has no contribution from zero. The cap does not require periodicity. Periodicity and odd-unit support enter the subsequent mean theorem. The native source and clock estimates discharge its limit hypotheses; it is not a conditional assertion of the marked limit.

The native inputs used here include `A.actual_unconditional_canonical_identification` in [ActualUnconditionalCanonicalTrace.lean](../formal-stationary-envelope/source/ActualUnconditionalCanonicalTrace.lean) and `P.exists_uniform_unit_canonical_trace_floor` in [CanonicalTracePolynomialFloor.lean](../formal-stationary-envelope/source/CanonicalTracePolynomialFloor.lean). The former supplies the signed Cesàro trace with the periodic Green factor. The latter combines the previously developed basin lower bound and comparison with weighted first-hit density. The actual real harmonic equation is transported to nonnegative extended reals by `E.actualIntegerTrace_harmonic` in [PadicBinaryEnvelopeTransfer.lean](../formal-stationary-envelope/source/PadicBinaryEnvelopeTransfer.lean). These arithmetic inputs are part of the included source closure and retain the upstream attribution described in the basin-density companion.

The infinite-value set is dense relative to the units, and its closure in the full 3-adic integer space is exactly the unit set. It is not dense in the full space, since the envelope vanishes on the clopen set of multiples of three. Infinite values at a dense set are compatible with finite values at every positive integer. The local unboundedness statement is a lower-existence assertion and supplies no arithmetic upper bound. The dyadic points used in its density proof are negative rational 3-adic units; the proof does not assert that every negative rational has infinite value.

It proves no individual forward-orbit convergence, cycle exclusion, or expected late-occupation bound.
