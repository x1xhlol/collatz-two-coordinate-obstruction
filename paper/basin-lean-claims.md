## Scope

The canonical stationarity theorem is an equality of Borel measures. It is not an assertion that a deterministic integer orbit has a stationary limiting distribution, nor an identification of a component-conditioned law with the canonical probability.

The global Green-sublinearity condition, zero mass of every divergent component, and universal eventual periodicity remain unproved. Their equivalence does not prove any one of them. Other cycles are not excluded. No theorem in this manuscript proves the Collatz conjecture.

The local theorem chain imports the native two-scale Tao, natural-density, and predecessor developments identified by the aggregate source manifest. The related-work discussion does not claim a local rebuild of the separately cited Shaik, Nashida, or Inselmann developments. The coverage claim concerns the manuscript’s 17 main mathematical statements and the explicit constructions listed above; it does not claim that every explanatory sentence or auxiliary display in every written proof has its own Lean declaration.
